What the Mixamo Blender plugin actually does
1) Renames and normalizes the Mixamo skeleton

Mixamo rigs come with:

long, inconsistent bone names

FBX-style hierarchy quirks

non-Blender-friendly conventions

The plugin:

renames bones to predictable patterns

fixes hierarchy oddities

makes the armature easier to work with in Blender

Key point:
👉 The skeleton is still the same Mixamo skeleton. No new bones, no repositioning.

2) Fixes orientation, roll, and rest-pose issues

Mixamo FBXs often import with:

strange bone roll

axis mismatches

rest pose vs pose pose confusion

The plugin:

corrects bone roll

aligns axes for Blender’s transform system

stabilizes rotations for animation editing

This is editor hygiene, not deformation logic.

3) Builds a Blender control rig (the big value)

This is the main reason people use it.

The plugin:

creates IK controls (hands, feet, spine)

adds pole targets

sets up constraints

lets animators pose the character without touching deform bones

Important:

Deform bones remain untouched

Controls drive the Mixamo bones via constraints

This is pure rig UI, not skinning

Think: Rigify-style controls layered on top of Mixamo.
