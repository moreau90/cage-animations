=== FULL BONE MAPPING: Mixamo FBX → Our Joint Names ===

Mixamo bone names describe the SEGMENT (limb part), but the bone POSITION
is at the JOINT where that segment begins. Our names describe the JOINT directly.

=== KEY DIFFERENCES FROM MIXAMO ===

1. HIPS vs HIP JOINTS:
   - Mixamo "Hips" = pelvis CENTER (root bone, high up at belt level)
   - Mixamo "LeftUpLeg"/"RightUpLeg" = hip joints, DOWN and to the SIDE from Hips
   - In Mixamo, hip joints are typically ~35mm BELOW Hips, and ~49mm to each side
   - OUR CODE: currently places hips at same Y as pelvis center (WRONG)
   - SHOULD BE: hips below pelvis, angled down-and-out like Mixamo

2. CLAVICLE (LeftShoulder/RightShoulder):
   - In Mixamo, these originate from Spine2 (upper spine), go OUT toward shoulders
   - They should angle outward and slightly forward from the spine
   - Our l_collar/r_collar may be derived incorrectly

3. NAMING CONVENTION:
   - Mixamo: segment-based names (the bone IS the segment)
   - Ours: joint-based names (the point IS the joint location)
   - Both refer to the same 3D position — just different naming philosophy
